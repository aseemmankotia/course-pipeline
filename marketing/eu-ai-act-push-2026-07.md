## Community answers (post as genuine replies where the question fits)

**r/privacy, r/ArtificialIntelligence, LinkedIn groups — "What happens August 2?"**

> August 2 is when the EU AI Act's obligations for high-risk systems become
> enforceable — conformity assessments, technical documentation, human
> oversight, logging, the lot. Fines scale to 3% of global turnover for
> high-risk violations (7% for prohibited practices). The practical gap most
> orgs have: nobody has classified their AI inventory into the Act's risk
> tiers yet, so they don't know which obligations apply. That inventory-and-
> classify exercise is the first thing to do, and it's genuinely a two-week
> job for a mid-size org, which is about the time remaining.
