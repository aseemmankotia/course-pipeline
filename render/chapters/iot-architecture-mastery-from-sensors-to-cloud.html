About This Course

The Internet of Things has evolved from simple device connectivity to a complex ecosystem of over 21 billion connected devices in 2026. This comprehensive course takes you beyond basic IoT concepts to master the architectural decisions that separate working prototypes from production-grade platforms.

You'll explore the complete IoT stack—from sensors and embedded systems through edge gateways, communication protocols, and cloud platforms. Through hands-on labs and real-world case studies, you'll learn to design systems that handle intermittent connectivity, scale to millions of devices, and maintain security across every layer. Whether you're building smart home products, industrial monitoring systems, or fleet management platforms, this course provides the architectural foundation you need.

By the end, you'll confidently make protocol selection decisions (MQTT vs CoAP vs AMQP), design edge-cloud hybrid architectures, implement device provisioning at scale, and build observability into your IoT solutions from day one.

What You'll Learn

Design end-to-end IoT architectures spanning device, gateway, and cloud layersSelect appropriate IoT protocols (MQTT, CoAP, AMQP, HTTP) based on use case requirementsImplement edge computing patterns for latency-sensitive applicationsConfigure device provisioning, identity management, and secure enrollmentBuild telemetry pipelines that scale from hundreds to millions of devicesDesign for IoT-specific failure modes including intermittent connectivityImplement device twin patterns for state synchronizationCreate observability and monitoring strategies for distributed IoT fleetsApply Zero Trust security principles to IoT deploymentsArchitect multi-tenant IoT platforms with proper isolation

Prerequisites

  • Basic understanding of networking concepts (TCP/IP, HTTP)
  • Familiarity with at least one programming language (Python recommended)
  • General awareness of cloud services (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
  • Basic knowledge of databases and APIs
