About This Course

This comprehensive course prepares you for the Microsoft AI-901: Azure AI Fundamentals certification exam - the updated 2026 version that replaces the AI-900. Whether you're a complete beginner with no technical background or a professional looking to validate your AI knowledge, this course covers everything you need to demonstrate foundational AI concepts and implement AI solutions using Microsoft Azure and the new Microsoft Foundry platform.

You'll explore artificial intelligence workloads, responsible AI principles, machine learning fundamentals, computer vision, natural language processing (NLP), and the exciting world of generative AI and agents. The course emphasizes practical skills needed to build modern AI apps using Foundry, with hands-on exercises that mirror real exam scenarios.

By the end of this course, you'll understand how to identify appropriate AI services for business scenarios, apply responsible AI principles, and confidently navigate Microsoft Foundry to implement AI solutions. The exam requires a passing score of 700/1000, and our structured approach - combining theory with practice - will give you the knowledge and confidence to succeed on your first attempt.
