About This Course

Generative AI has evolved beyond simple text generation into sophisticated agentic systems that can autonomously plan, reason, and take action. This course bridges the gap between understanding large language models and building production-ready AI agents that deliver real-world value.

You'll learn the foundational patterns that power modern agentic AI—from ReAct and tool use to multi-agent orchestration—while gaining hands-on experience with industry-leading frameworks like LangChain, CrewAI, and the OpenAI Agents SDK. By the end of this course, you'll have built deployable AI agents capable of coordinating complex workflows, integrating with external tools, and making autonomous decisions within safe guardrails.

Designed for intermediate developers, this course emphasizes practical implementation over theory. You'll work with real code, deploy actual agents, and develop the systems-thinking mindset that separates hobby projects from enterprise-grade agentic solutions. Whether you're building intelligent copilots, automating business workflows, or creating autonomous research assistants, this course provides the architectural foundation you need to succeed in the agentic AI era of 2026.
