About This Course

Artificial intelligence is transforming every industry, from healthcare to entertainment, and understanding its fundamentals has become an essential skill for professionals in any field. This beginner-friendly course demystifies AI and machine learning, breaking down complex concepts into digestible, engaging lessons that anyone can understand.

You'll discover what AI can and cannot do, learn how machines actually learn from data, and explore the different types of machine learning that power everything from Netflix recommendations to medical diagnoses. Through real-world examples and hands-on activities, you'll build the foundational knowledge needed to understand AI's impact on your work and daily life.

By the end of this course, you'll have a solid conceptual understanding of AI and machine learning fundamentals, be able to identify AI opportunities and limitations, and be prepared to continue your learning journey into more technical AI topics if you choose.
